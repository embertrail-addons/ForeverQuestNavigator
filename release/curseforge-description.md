Forever Quest Navigator (FQN) helps you choose your next quest using distance, level suitability, and known follow-up quests.

Built for World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1.

- See your active quest, objective progress, approximate XP, and nearby alternatives in a compact tracker.
- Follow a movable direction arrow with the quest name, current objective, and distance.
- Navigate to the closest known unfinished objective, choose a target yourself, or temporarily avoid it.
- Consider known follow-up quests and nearby stops when planning an outing.
- Discover nearby quests to accept through Questie, with level, distance, and recommendation details.
- Open quest destinations on the map and place a removable FQN marker.
- Skip accepted quests without abandoning them. Skips are saved per character, and the most recent skip can be undone.
- Browse a local overview of objectives and turn-ins with known positions.
- Adjust opacity, text size, window width, visible list entries, and positions. English and German are included.

**Getting started**

Install FQN for the Forever client and enter the world. Use the gold FQN minimap button to switch between the full tracker, arrow only, and hidden. Right-click the minimap button to open settings, or type `/fqn settings`.

Use `/fqn` to toggle the tracker and `/fqn status` to check Questie's status.

**Questie integration**

FQN reads the game's quest log and map information. Install the Forever versions of Questie and QuestieDB for objective spawn locations, nearby quest offers, and supported follow-up planning. These addons are separate downloads.

**Route and XP estimates**

Distances use straight-line measurements. The displayed route depends on available quest and map data. XP figures are estimates when shown with ~. Follow-up planning considers up to two known, unambiguous follow-up quests.

Outdoor elite and group quests are included. Dungeon, raid, and PvP objectives are excluded from normal recommendations. FQN displays recommendations and waypoints while you accept, complete, and turn in quests through the game.
