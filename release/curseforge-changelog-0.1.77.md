# Forever Quest Navigator 0.1.77

Local development build; not published.

- Nearby offers distinguish pickup distance from objective destination, with detailed pickup-to-objective distances.
- Route-fit and shared-area labels explain why an offer fits. Unknown positions, unsuitable levels and entrance-only measurements cannot create strong pickup advice.
- Before-departure advice highlights up to three useful nearby pickups, limited by free quest-log slots and the known follow-up reserve.
- Quest planning adds expandable destination clusters with contributing active quests, required pickups, known XP and missing-XP notices.
- An advisory next round lists pickups before objectives, accounts for known follow-up destinations and can suggest a nearby ready hand-in when no pickup slots remain.
- Plans are coordinate-based suggestions, not verified walking routes or guaranteed shared enemies. No quests are automatically accepted, abandoned or skipped.
