Version 0.1.55 beta.

- Expanded quest tooltips explain target distance, level suitability, available follow-up routes, and manual selection.
- More stable distance ordering with an adaptive switching margin.
- Nearby offers distinguish pickup distance from the objective location and travel after accepting.
- Unknown levels, objective locations, XP and follow-up routes are handled explicitly and conservatively.
- Added /fqn debug: a copyable diagnostic snapshot with quest IDs, target coordinates, distance scores and selection reasons.
- Shared quest row layout, highlights and tooltip handling.
